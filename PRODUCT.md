# Product

## Register

product

## Users

StudyDeck AI serves school pupils, college students, university students, and teachers who need to turn topics, notes, PDFs, articles, and class materials into clear study presentations. Students usually arrive with a deadline, incomplete source material, and a need to understand the subject well enough to present it.

## Product Purpose

The product helps users structure learning material into a presentation with a plan, editable slides, source references, speaker notes, a slide-by-slide speech script, and PDF/PPTX export. It should feel like a study assistant, not a tool that silently does homework for the user.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, clear, supportive. The interface should feel productive and friendly for students without becoming childish, gamified for its own sake, or visually noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic green SaaS styling, childish classroom cartoons, decorative AI gradients, heavy glassmorphism, oversized marketing hero layouts, and interfaces that hide source quality behind polished slides.

## Design Principles

Start from the student's next action.
Show the workflow from prompt to export as a visible progression.
Make source confidence and speaker preparation first-class parts of the editor.
Use color as task language: orange for action, green for verified progress, purple for AI/editor power.
Keep the product familiar enough to trust and energetic enough to feel made for study.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, long Russian labels, small mobile screens, and users who need clear recovery from empty, loading, weak-source, and error states.
